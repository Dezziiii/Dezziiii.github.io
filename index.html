(01) — About

I believe great games are built where space and character meet — a level that teaches you how to play, and a character that makes you want to.

I’m a future game designer focused on level design and character design. I care about pacing, readability, and the small moments of delight that turn a room into a memory.

My approach is part architect, part storyteller: blocking out spaces that guide players intuitively, then populating them with characters who have history, intent, and a point of view.
