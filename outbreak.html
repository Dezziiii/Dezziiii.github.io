Outbreak.

A survival-horror level built in Unreal Engine 5. You start at a military outpost carved into a canyon and work your way down into the lab where the outbreak began.

It leans on light, landmarks, and pacing to move you through the dark — no markers, no minimap.

Further down, this overview walks through the early concepts and layouts of the level — the blockouts, before the final refinements. Seeing them next to the finished spaces is what really shows the iterative process of improvement behind it.
