Writing ::

My corner of the internet — a place to think out loud about game design and the small decisions that make a game click.

I write to understand. Documenting a blockout or a design choice forces me to notice why it works — or why it doesn't.

If a note here helps you look at a game a little differently, that's the whole point.

Click a log to read it.

My first time working on a team project — it was definitely something to remember. It was a small, five-man team. Granted, not everyone was putting in 100%, but I know I was, and I genuinely enjoyed working with everyone nonetheless.

A team environment really shows how you work with others, and it let me see how I work in a team setting in game design rather than in other day-to-day things. We were able to communicate our problems effectively and work without stepping on each other's toes.

At the very end of our Zombie Outbreak project, it turned out absolutely amazing — and I'm very happy about it.
